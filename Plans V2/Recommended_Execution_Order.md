## Recommended Execution Order

1. **Pipeline contracts and artifact versioning**
2. **Promise, research, story, hook, and retention engine**
3. **Scene Director and timing compiler**
4. **Core Remotion components and kinetic captions**
5. **Dynamic audio and sound design**
6. **Quality gate and real Telegram publication approval**
7. **Publish-package alignment**
8. **Analytics ingestion and learning loop**

For an immediate visible improvement, the team can build `BigNumber`, `FullScreenTypography`, `Chart`, `CinematicMedia`, and phrase-level captions in parallel with the foundation work. The full component library should wait until the Scene Director contract is stable, or substantial renderer rework is likely.
